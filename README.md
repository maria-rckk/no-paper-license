# The No Paper License

A permissive software license for almost everyone.

The No Paper License is based on the MIT License, with one material change: Paper, publicly known by the online identity `@paper`, people or entities materially associated with Paper, and anything that is, was, became, came from, contains, or may reasonably be described as Paper receive no rights to the software.

The associated-party language is intentionally broad for the joke: founders, owners, employees, contractors, agents, subsidiaries, parent entities, successors, predecessors, rebrands, spinouts, former or current manifestations of Paper, and similar Paper-adjacent parties are covered.

## Use it

Copy [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) into your repository, then replace:

- `[year]` with the current year
- `[copyright holder]` with your name or organization

If your project uses SPDX metadata, use a custom identifier such as:

```text
LicenseRef-No-Paper-1.0
```

## Is this open source?

No. A license that excludes a person, account, company, or associated group does not meet the Open Source Definition. Call the software **source-available under the No Paper License**.

## Why?

Because paper is the natural enemy of rock, and apparently named-exclusion licenses are a genre now.

## Important

This is a joke license published for cultural and comedic purposes. The associated-party language is deliberately silly and broad, not a claim about any particular person or organization. If you need terms you expect to enforce, have a qualified lawyer review them in your jurisdiction.
